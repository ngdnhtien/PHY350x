# Literature

The primary sources behind each method used in the course, in story order.
No PDFs are redistributed here (most are not freely licensed); `references.bib`
holds the same entries in BibTeX form and is what `report/main.tex` cites.

## Part I — Iterate

- **Newton–Raphson for systems.** T. J. Ypma, *Historical development of the Newton–Raphson method*, SIAM Review **37**, 531 (1995). https://doi.org/10.1137/1037125
- **Gauss–Seidel iteration.** Y. Saad, *Iterative Methods for Sparse Linear Systems*, 2nd ed. (SIAM, 2003), Ch. 4. Free PDF from the author: https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~saad/IterMethBook_2ndEd.pdf
- **The QR algorithm.** J. G. F. Francis, *The QR transformation, parts I and II*, Computer J. **4**, 265 and 332 (1961/62). https://doi.org/10.1093/comjnl/4.3.265 ; V. N. Kublanovskaya, USSR Comput. Math. Math. Phys. **1**, 637 (1962).
- General reference: G. H. Golub and C. F. Van Loan, *Matrix Computations*, 4th ed. (Johns Hopkins, 2013), Ch. 7.

## Part II — Sample

- **The Monte Carlo method.** N. Metropolis and S. Ulam, J. Am. Stat. Assoc. **44**, 335 (1949). https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1949.10483310
- **Linear congruential generators (the "minimal standard" a = 16807, m = 2³¹−1).** S. K. Park and K. W. Miller, *Random number generators: good ones are hard to find*, Commun. ACM **31**, 1192 (1988). https://doi.org/10.1145/63039.63042
- **Box–Muller transform.** G. E. P. Box and M. E. Muller, Ann. Math. Stat. **29**, 610 (1958). https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177706645
- **Rejection sampling.** J. von Neumann, *Various techniques used in connection with random digits*, NBS Appl. Math. Ser. **12**, 36 (1951).
- **Radioactive decay chains (Bateman equations).** H. Bateman, Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. **15**, 423 (1910).
- **Poisson statistics of decay.** E. Rutherford and H. Geiger (with a note by H. Bateman), *The probability variations in the distribution of α particles*, Philos. Mag. **20**, 698 (1910). https://doi.org/10.1080/14786441008636955

## Part III — Evolve

- **Hard-sphere / kinetic gas simulation.** B. J. Alder and T. E. Wainwright, *Studies in molecular dynamics. I*, J. Chem. Phys. **31**, 459 (1959). https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1730376
- **Crank–Nicolson.** J. Crank and P. Nicolson, Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. **43**, 50 (1947). https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100023197
- **Stability of explicit schemes (the γ ≤ ½ condition).** R. Courant, K. Friedrichs and H. Lewy, Math. Ann. **100**, 32 (1928). https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01448839 ; J. Crank, *The Mathematics of Diffusion*, 2nd ed. (Oxford, 1975), Ch. 8.
